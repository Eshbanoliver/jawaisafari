## Brand & Visual Philosophy
**JAWAI SAFARI HUB** is designed as a luxury wilderness expedition website celebrating the raw majesty of Jawai's prehistoric granite kopjes, wild Indian leopards (*Panthera pardus fusca*), marsh crocodile wetlands at Jawai Dam, and the sacred centuries-old coexistence with the indigenous Rabari pastoralist shepherds.

## Key Features & Architecture
- **Cinematic Full-Screen Hero:** Golden-hour leopard on monolithic granite formation, subtle coordinates, and animated scroll indicator.
- **Asymmetric Editorial Introduction:** Historical narrative on Jawai's granite topography and Rabari harmony.
- **Safari Experiences:**
  1. *Leopard Safari* (Tracking on granite kopjes)
  2. *Jawai Jeep Safari* (Open 4x4 rugged terrain driving)
  3. *Wildlife & Nature Experience* (Jawai Dam wetlands, marsh crocodiles & migratory birds)
- **Safari Packages:** Three customizable luxury packages with direct contextual WhatsApp booking links.
- **Interactive Wildlife Gallery:** Categorized masonry gallery with high-resolution lightbox image viewer.
- **Direct WhatsApp & Phone Integration:** Every enquiry button automatically generates context-specific WhatsApp messages for instant customer conversion.
- **Google Maps Integration:** Embedded verified business location iframe for Jawai Safari Hub.
- **SEO & Structured Data:** Open Graph, Twitter cards, meta descriptions, sitemap.xml, robots.txt, and LocalBusiness JSON-LD schema.

## Replacing Media & Customizing Content
All photographs, captions, and categories are centrally managed in `src/data/images.ts`. To replace photographs with original on-ground safari assets, update the URLs or add local image files inside `src/assets/`.
Package details and business coordinates are centrally maintained in `src/data/safariData.ts`.
